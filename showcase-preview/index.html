WHAT SUNBIRD RC CONTRIBUTES

A foundation ecosystems configure for themselves

Authorities define the records, identifiers, governance, credentials, trust and service rules. Sunbird RC supplies reusable Registry and Credential building blocks.

  1. 01Configurable domain registries
  2. 02Independent credential issuers

  4. 04Holder consent and selective disclosure
  5. 05Reusable verification before domain decisions
