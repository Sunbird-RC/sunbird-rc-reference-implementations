TWO THINGS THAT ARE NOT DONE, recorded rather than left to be discovered
  1. The Authority admin API runs here with ENABLE_AUTH=false. It is published on
     127.0.0.1 only, so it is not reachable from the internet, but it is not the
     authenticated surface it should be. Authenticated operation needs a Keycloak
     `authority` realm, which does not exist, AND token acquisition in the oid4vc
     claim source, which today reads a static AUTHORITY_TOKEN and cannot refresh
     one. Neither is a large piece of work; neither is done.
  2. The Authority's public trust routes are not published through the gateway.
     The verifier reaches them on the internal network, which is why the suite is
     green, but a relying party outside this deployment currently cannot check
     status. Publishing them is a new public surface and was not taken
     unilaterally.
