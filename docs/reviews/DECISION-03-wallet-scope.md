# Decision 03 — Wallet scope for the demo programme

**Decision owner:** Anand

**Decision date:** 31 August 2026

**Status:** Approved and effective across all current demo iterations

## Decision

Inji is removed from the scope of the Sunbird RC demo programme.

Age, Agriculture, and Education use the established customized Paradym-based
open-source wallet. No current or later demo iteration owes an Inji build,
compatibility handshake, interoperability proof, or customer demonstration.

## Consequences

- Existing Inji research is retained only as historical context.
- It must not be treated as an open acceptance item, deferred requirement, risk
  assigned to Education, or reason to delay an iteration.
- The demos make no claim that Inji interoperates with this implementation.
- A future Inji evaluation would require a new explicit Product decision and a
  separately approved scope.

This decision supersedes every earlier statement requiring Inji to complete at
least one use case or deferring that requirement to Iteration 03.
